## Overview

An EMS (Energy Management System) that complements and extends Home Assistant. It's installable as a Home Assistant app and integrates with Home Assistant's devices and integrations.

Open source, for anyone to use. Long-term goal is to grow it into a small business.

## Constraints

- Requires Home Assistant — it's an app for it, not a standalone product.

## Status

Building the MVP: the two V1 features in [roadmap.md](roadmap.md). Once done, testing them out in the field.
